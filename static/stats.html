📊 Live Detection Performance

These numbers are computed live — the engine runs over every transaction in the dataset right now and scores against the known fraud labels. Nothing here is hard-coded.

–
Precision
Of the payments we flagged, how many were actually fraud. High precision = few false alarms.
–
Recall
Of all the real fraud in the data, how much the shield caught. High recall = few scams slip through.
–
False-positive rate
Share of legitimate payments the shield wrongly flagged. Kept low so honest users aren't blocked.

Why these numbers are honest. The test data is synthetic and deliberately varied — fraud trips only a random subset of signals, so some scams look almost normal. That is why a few are missed and a few legitimate payments are flagged. A perfect score on synthetic data would be a red flag; these realistic trade-offs are exactly what the bank review console exists to catch and correct. Voice-phishing detection adds a second, independent layer on top of these payment-behaviour results.
